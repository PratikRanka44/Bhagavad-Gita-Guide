A verse, not a guess

Most AI tools asked about scripture will misquote it — wrong verse numbers, invented Sanskrit, blended translations. Gita Guide is built around one rule: the model never generates the source text. Every verse shown is retrieved verbatim from a verified 719-verse dataset covering all 18 chapters. The AI only writes the explanation layered on top.

Bhagavad Gita 2.47
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥
karmaṇy evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana...
"You have a right to your actions, but never to the fruits of those actions..."

What's inside

Full corpus

All 719 verses across all 18 chapters — Sanskrit, IAST transliteration, Hindi, and English.

Four languages

Get guidance in English, Hindi, or Marathi. Type your situation in any of them too.

Handles real & reflective questions

"I'm scared to change careers" and "what is the meaning of happiness" both work — trivia and off-topic requests are politely declined.

Verified sourcing

Verse text comes from an open, citable dataset — not generated by an LLM. See the GitHub repo for full attribution.
